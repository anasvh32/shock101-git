## 3. Alamat-ha

## 4. Tasmim

## 5. Kar-ha
Aval `experiment/tone` ro sakhtam, javabe rasmi ro neveshtam va commit kardam. bad bargashtam roye `main` va javabe khodmani ro gozashtam. bad merge kardam. Conflict oomad, aval eshtebahan Accept Both zadam, bad khate ezafe ro pak kardam va faghat khodmani ro gozashtam va merge ro commit kardam.

## 6. Dars
Fahmidam Conflict vaghti pish miad ke do ta branch roye ye khat az ye file taghire motafavet dade bashan. va bad az hale Conflict bayad hatman commit koni, vagarné merge nimekare mimoone.
